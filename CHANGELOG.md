# What's new in ReaderCount

## 1.0.0: Free on your own Cloudflare
_2026-11-04_

- Free and source available: ReaderCount now runs on your own Cloudflare account, free for one person or organisation's own websites, up to 10. Every feature is included, with no licence key or trial.
- Sign in with your own password: one owner account, set with the ADMIN_PASSWORD secret.
- Your own brand: your name, logo, colour and support address on every page and email.
- Client mode: agencies and hosts can offer stats to their clients, with their own plans and payments to their own Stripe account. Needs a commercial licence.
- Three ways to install: the installer (npx @mrpwob/readercount setup), the Deploy to Cloudflare button or from the code.
